## Hi there! 👋

I'm **Diana**, an AI researcher and PhD candidate based in Romania.

I work on generative AI for clinical decision support, mostly diffusion models
for cardiac imaging and ECG. My recent work includes **EchoCF**, a model that
generates counterfactual echocardiograms conditioned on ejection fraction, and
diffusion-based data augmentation for cardiac MRI segmentation.

I'm doing my PhD at the Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy (IMAR),
I'm a researcher on a medical AI project at the University of Craiova, where I
also teach labs in data analysis, databases and computer graphics. Before that,
I spent 2.5 years at **HELLA (FORVIA)** testing automotive software, which is
where I learned to care about code that actually works.

I'm interested in models that doctors can trust: generative methods that are
controllable, anatomically plausible and honest about their limits.

Outside of research, I'm into photography, fashion and video games.
